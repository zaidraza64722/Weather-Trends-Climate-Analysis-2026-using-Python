# Business Insights Summary
## Weather Trends and Climate Analysis – 2026

### Project Overview
This analysis explores historical weather data from five Indian cities:
- Bengaluru
- Mumbai
- Delhi
- Chennai
- Hyderabad

**Analysis Period:** January 1 – October 30, 2026

### Key Areas of Analysis

1. Average Temperature Comparison
2. Total Rainfall Comparison
3. Monthly Temperature Trends
4. Average Wind Speed Comparison
5. Highest and Lowest Temperature Recorded
6. Number of Rainy Days
7. Temperature Variation Across Cities
8. Average Wind Gust Comparison
9. Monthly Rainfall Comparison

### Business Recommendations
- Monitor temperature patterns across cities.
- Use rainfall trends for water resource planning.
- Monitor wind speed and gust patterns.
- Study seasonal weather variations.
- Improve weather awareness and preparedness.

### Conclusion
The project uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to explore weather patterns, compare five Indian cities, and identify meaningful trends.
